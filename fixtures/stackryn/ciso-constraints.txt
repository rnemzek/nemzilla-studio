CISO COMPLIANCE CONSTRAINTS — PROJECT HORIZON

1. Zero local PII storage — no customer PII may be persisted outside the
   vendor-hosted CRM/identity layer.
2. SOC2 Type II certification required for all new services prior to
   production traffic.
3. Authentication must use SAML 2.0 via Okta SSO — no local username/
   password auth paths permitted.
4. Audit logs must be retained for a minimum of 7 years and be tamper-
   evident (hash-chained).
5. Any offline/batch sync path (e.g. AS400 nightly export) must be
   reconciled against the real-time system of record before use in
   financial reporting.
