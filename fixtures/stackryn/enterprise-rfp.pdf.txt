PROJECT HORIZON — CORE ERP & BILLING MODERNIZATION
Request for Proposal (Executive Summary — extracted via PDF text layer)

Sponsor: VP of Enterprise Technology
Objective: Replace the legacy AS400-driven order-to-cash pipeline and Oracle
DB v11g billing warehouse with a modern, API-first platform integrated with
Salesforce, Stripe, and Snowflake.

Scope:
- Real-time billing and portal modernization for 40,000+ active accounts.
- Zero local PII storage — all customer PII must reside in the vendor-hosted
  identity/CRM layer.
- SOC2 Type II compliance is mandatory for go-live.
- Target go-live: Q3 next fiscal year.

Budget Guidance: $420,000 - $480,000
Requested Timeline: 16-20 weeks from kickoff.
